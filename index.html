(A) Zoology (जन्तु विज्ञान) Sec B
  • Non-Chordata: Entamoeba, Plasmodium, Paramoecium, Euglena, Leucosolenia, Sycon, Hydra, Obelia, Aurelia, Fasciola, Taenia, Ascaris, Nereis, Pheretima, Leech, Cockroach, Musca, Mosquito, Palaemon, Unio, Pila, Starfish.
  • Protochordata & Chordata: Herdmania, Amphioxus, Scoliodon, Rana tigrina, Uromastix, Columba, Oryctolagus.
  • Cell Biology & Genetics: Mitosis, Meiosis, Gametogenesis, Mendelism, Linkage, Crossing Over, Eugenics.
  • Evolution, Ecology & Physiology: Lamarckism, Darwinism, Digestion, Respiration, Circulation, Excretion, Nervous & Endocrine, Biochemistry, Embryology (Amphioxus, Frog, Chick), Zoogeography.
(B) Botany (वनस्पति विज्ञान) Sec B
  • Cryptogams & Gymnosperms: Viruses, Lichens, Algae (Nostoc, Volvox, Oedogonium, Ectocarpus, Batrachospermum), Fungi (Rhizopus, Albugo, Puccinia, Agaricus), Bryophyta (Riccia, Marchantia, Funaria), Pteridophyta (Lycopodium, Selaginella, Equisetum, Marsilea), Cycas, Pinus & Palaeobotany.
  • Taxonomy (Bentham & Hooker): 14 Families (Ranunculaceae, Brassicaceae, Fabaceae, Asteraceae, Solanaceae, Lamiaceae, Liliaceae, Poaceae, etc.) & Economic Botany.
  • Anatomy & Embryology: Anomalous secondary growth (Tinospora, Dracaena, Boerhaavia, Nyctanthes), Micro/Megasporogenesis.
  • Plant Physiology & Soil Science: Photosynthesis, Respiration, N2 Cycle, Hormones.

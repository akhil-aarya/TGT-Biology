Section A: General Knowledge (30 Qs)

Unit 1: Current Events (State, National, International), Personalities, Sports.

Unit 2: Indian History & National Movement, Polity & Educational Constitutional provisions, Indian Economy, Geography & Population.

Unit 3: General Science, Environment, Human Health (Vitamins, Diseases, Hormones, Enzymes), Computer, ICT & Educational Tech.

Section B1: Zoology (45 Qs)

Non-Chordata & Chordata: Entamoeba, Plasmodium, Paramoecium, Euglena, Leucosolenia, Sycon, Hydra, Obelia, Aurelia, Fasciola, Taenia, Ascaris, Nereis, Pheretima, Leech, Cockroach, Musca, Mosquito, Palaemon, Unio, Pila, Starfish, Herdmania, Amphioxus, Scoliodon, Rana, Uromastix, Columba, Oryctolagus.

Core Units: Cell Biology, Genetics, Evolution, Ecology & Pollution, Physiology, Biochemistry, Embryology (Amphioxus, Frog, Chick, Placenta) & Zoogeography.

Section B2: Botany (45 Qs)

Plant Diversity: Viruses, Lichens, Algae (Nostoc, Chlamydomonas, Volvox, Ulothrix, Spirogyra, Oedogonium, Ectocarpus, Batrachospermum), Fungi (Mucor, Rhizopus, Pythium, Albugo, Saccharomyces, Penicillium, Puccinia, Agaricus), Bryophyta (Riccia, Marchantia, Funaria), Pteridophyta (Lycopodium, Selaginella, Equisetum, Marsilea), Gymnosperms (Cycas, Pinus), Palaeobotany.

Angiosperms & Physiology: Bentham & Hooker + 15 Families, Economic Botany, Anomalous Secondary Growth (Tinospora, Dracaena, Achyranthes, Boerhaavia, Nyctanthes), Embryology, Ecology, Cytogenetics, Plant Physiology & Soil Science.
